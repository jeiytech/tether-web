TETHER

Privacy Policy · Last updated May 2026

Tether is built on a privacy-first principle. Your location is never tracked globally. It is only used during active hangouts, only with people in that hangout, and never stored as a history.

What We Collect

  • Your name and email address (from Google or Apple sign-in)
  • A username you choose
  • Hangout data you create (title, location, time)
  • Photos and media you upload to memory archives
  • Proximity signals (BLE and GPS) during active hangouts only

What We Do Not Collect

  • Persistent location history
  • Global tracking outside of active hangouts
  • Browsing or activity data
  • Contacts or address book

How Location Works

Location access is requested only when you open a live hangout. Your GPS coordinates are sent to our server, which computes your distance from the venue. Raw coordinates are never returned to the app or shown to other users.

How We Use Your Data

  • To operate the app and validate physical presence at hangouts
  • To create shared memory archives for hangout participants
  • To let friends find you by username or email

Your Rights

  • Delete your account at any time from Settings
  • Request a copy of your data by emailing us
  • Opt out of location sharing per hangout
